# Where to find deals

*Rendered on the Silvercurrent home screen. To update, edit this file — no redeploy needed.*

This is your deal-sourcing playbook. Sign up for the alerts, point them at your Silvercurrent inbox, and the pipeline does the rest. The list is organized by funnel quality — start at the top.

---

## Tier 1 — Broad aggregators (set up email alerts immediately)

These are where ~80% of your deal flow will come from. Sign up for accounts, configure saved searches matching your criteria (industry, geography, SDE band), enable email alerts, and forward those alerts to your Silvercurrent ingestion address.

- **BizBuySell** (`bizbuysell.com`) — the largest US marketplace, ~45k active listings. Saved-search alerts are reliable. Their *Insight Reports* (free quarterly) also feed the Analyst agent's comp data.
- **BizQuest** (`bizquest.com`) — second-largest. Significant overlap with BizBuySell but worth dual-coverage; some brokers list on one and not the other.
- **BusinessesForSale.com** — global; filter to US. Smaller US footprint but catches some listings the big two miss.
- **BusinessBroker.net** — broker-aggregator, decent volume.
- **LoopNet** — primarily commercial real estate but has a "Businesses for Sale" tab. Useful for deals where the real estate is a meaningful part of the value (laundromats, restaurants with owned premises).
- **DealStream** (`dealstream.com`, formerly MergerNetwork) — middle-market leaning, includes some off-market opportunities.

## Tier 2 — Online / digital businesses

Skip these if you're targeting a Main Street operating business. Subscribe if you'd consider a SaaS, e-commerce, or content business.

- **Acquire.com** — SaaS and digital businesses; vetted, with structured financials.
- **Empire Flippers** — premium online businesses, strong vetting.
- **Quiet Light Brokerage** — premium online, often higher quality.
- **FE International** — premium online, larger deals.
- **Flippa** — high volume, low quality floor; useful for breadth, not depth.

## Tier 3 — Broker networks (good for proprietary access)

Brokers in these networks often pocket-list before public posting. Building a relationship with even one local broker in your geography is worth more than ten saved searches.

- **Sunbelt Network** — largest US broker network; find your local office.
- **Transworld Business Advisors** — large broker network.
- **Murphy Business & Financial** — broker network.
- **IBBA (International Business Brokers Association)** — member directory at `ibba.org`. The Certified Business Intermediary (CBI) credential is meaningful.
- **M&A Source** — slightly larger deal sizes than IBBA, also a credential program (M&AMI).

## Tier 4 — Search / ETA-specific resources

- **Searchfunder.com** — community for searchers; deals shared informally, plus mentor connections and resources. Free to join.
- **Acquiring Minds podcast** (`acquiringminds.co`) — interviews with searchers and operators; shownotes often link to deals or brokers.
- **Think Like an Owner podcast** — same genre; deeper on operating once acquired.
- **Stanford Search Fund Primer** (free PDF, GSB website) — canonical text on search fund mechanics. Lives in the Operator agent's knowledge base.

## Tier 5 — Off-market (where the best deals actually live)

These don't fit the email-alert pipeline but should be on your radar — and Silvercurrent's URL paste / PDF upload flows are designed for exactly the unstructured listings you'll get from these sources.

- **Direct owner outreach** — pick an industry and geography, build a list from Google Maps + state Secretary of State filings + LinkedIn, send polite letters. Highest-effort, highest-quality channel for serious searchers.
- **Local CPAs, estate attorneys, commercial bankers** — they know which of their clients are nearing retirement before listings exist. Coffee meetings convert.
- **Trade associations & industry publications** — most industries have one; many have classified sections or member-only listings.
- **Chamber of commerce events** — slow but high signal in a specific geography.

---

## Recommended starting setup

For your first month using Silvercurrent:

1. Set alerts on **BizBuySell + BizQuest + BusinessesForSale + LoopNet** with your criteria.
2. Subscribe to **Searchfunder** and the **Acquiring Minds** podcast.
3. Forward all alert emails to your Silvercurrent inbox address.

That alone will saturate your screening capacity. Add Tier 3 (broker relationships) once you have a clearer geographic and industry focus.
