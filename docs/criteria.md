## Notes for the Analyst agent

When scoring a listing against this document:

- Treat **Hard nos** and **Hard knockouts** as binary fails. Flag the listing prominently and recommend "pass" as next action.
- Treat **Targets**, **Primary geography**, and the **Operating profile** numerics as scoring weights — closer to target = higher score.
- Treat **Soft preferences** as positive modifiers but not deal-makers on their own.
- When a listing doesn't disclose the data needed to evaluate a criterion, mark it "not disclosed" in the fit assessment — never extrapolate.
